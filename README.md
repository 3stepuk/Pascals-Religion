# Pascal's Religion — Port-Royal, grace, and a condemned movement

Six sessions on what Blaise Pascal actually held: the night of 23 November 1654,
Port-Royal, Jansenism and the condemnations of 1653 and after, the hidden God,
the machine, and what a Catholic may take from him.

Not core Catholicism. Jansenism was condemned. This is a study of a contested
movement, not a recommendation.
